# First Client Offer

## Offer Name

Tri-Cities Website Rescue Audit

## Simple Promise

Digital Helper will find the clearest online visibility or conversion problem costing the business calls, leads, or trust.

## Entry Offer

Free 3 to 5 point audit.

If there is a clear quick win:

> Digital Helper fixes one high-impact issue for $497.

Examples:

- Create or repair a focused homepage section.
- Add a clear quote/contact path.
- Improve service page copy.
- Fix obvious mobile layout problems.
- Clean up Google Business Profile descriptions.
- Add local SEO structure for one service/city.

## Upsell Path

After the $497 fix:

| Need | Offer |
| --- | --- |
| Website is missing or outdated | Website rebuild starting at $2,500 |
| Business does not show locally | Local SEO starting at $750/month |
| Leads are missed after hours | AI chat or workflow automation starting at $497/month |
| Business needs predictable pipeline | Lead generation pilot |

## Why This Offer

- It is small enough for a first client to say yes.
- It proves Digital Helper can help before asking for a large project.
- It creates a natural next conversation.
- It gives Mars a paid proof point without needing a big case study first.

## Close Script

```text
The biggest quick win I see is [specific issue].

I can fix that first for $497 so you are not committing to a full rebuild before seeing how I work.

If that goes well, I can quote the larger website or SEO work after.
```
