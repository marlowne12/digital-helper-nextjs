# Money Plan

## The Fastest Path To Cash

Sell one small, concrete outcome to a local service business:

> Stop missing leads from your website, Google profile, or after-hours calls.

Do not start by selling a full agency package. Start with a paid starter fix.

## Offer To Sell First

### $497 Lead Capture Fix

For a local service business, Digital Helper will fix one obvious place where leads are being lost.

Examples:

- Add or repair the website contact/quote path.
- Add a simple AI lead-capture widget or intake flow.
- Rewrite the homepage/service copy so people know what to do next.
- Clean up the Google Business Profile service description.
- Add a direct call/quote CTA and lead notification flow.

### Why This Is The First Offer

- It is cheaper than a full website rebuild.
- It is easy for an owner to understand.
- It gives Mars a paid first win.
- It creates a natural upsell to monthly AI automation, web design, or SEO.

## Who To Contact First

Target local businesses where one new job is worth enough to justify the fix:

1. HVAC
2. Plumbing
3. Roofing
4. Electrical
5. Landscaping

Avoid restaurants, retail, and low-ticket businesses until there is proof.

## What To Say

Lead with a specific missed-lead problem:

```text
I noticed [specific issue]. For a business like yours, that can mean people search, compare, and call someone else before you ever hear from them.

I can send you a quick free audit. If there is a simple fix, I can handle the first one for $497 so you do not have to commit to a full rebuild.
```
