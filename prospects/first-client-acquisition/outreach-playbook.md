# Outreach Playbook

## Positioning

Digital Helper helps Tri-Cities businesses turn weak online presence into calls, quote requests, and booked work.

Lead with one useful observation, not "we are a web design agency."

## First Email

Subject options:

- Quick website idea for [Business Name]
- Noticed something on Google for [Business Name]
- Small idea to help [Business Name] get more calls

Email:

```text
Hi [First Name],

I was looking at local [category] businesses in [city] and came across [Business Name].

One thing I noticed: [specific observation].

That can matter because people searching for [service] usually decide quickly who looks trustworthy enough to call. A clearer website or Google profile can make that decision easier.

I run Digital Helper here in the Tri-Cities. If it helps, I can send a short free audit with 3 practical fixes for [Business Name]. No pitch required.

Best,
Mars
Digital Helper
(509) 987-5060
https://digital-helper.com
```

## Phone Opener

```text
Hi, is this [Business Name]?

My name is Mars. I run Digital Helper here in the Tri-Cities.

I was looking at local [category] businesses and noticed [specific observation]. I am not calling with a big sales pitch. I wanted to offer a quick free website or Google visibility audit with a few fixes you can use either way.

Who would be the right person to send that to?
```

## Follow-Up After No Reply

```text
Hi [First Name],

Quick follow-up on this. The main thing I noticed for [Business Name] was [specific issue].

Even one small improvement, like [simple fix], could make it easier for local customers to call or request a quote.

Want me to send the short audit?

Mars
(509) 987-5060
```

## Warm Reply Response

```text
Thanks, [First Name].

I will take a quick look and send back 3 to 5 practical notes. I will keep it focused on changes that could help people find you, trust you, or contact you faster.

If there is an obvious quick win, I can also price that separately so you do not have to commit to a full rebuild right away.
```

## Manual Personalization Checklist

- Business name is correct.
- City is correct.
- Category/service is correct.
- Observation is real.
- Message offers help before asking for money.
- Phone number is `(509) 987-5060`.
