## Gig 1: Custom AI Automation Skill

Title:
I will build a custom AI automation skill for your workflow

Category:
Programming & Tech -> AI Development or AI Agents

Tags:
openclaw, ai automation, custom skill, workflow automation, ai assistant

Packages:
- Basic, Simple Skill, $29, 2 days, 1 revision: one-input one-output SKILL.md, README, tested sample output
- Standard, Workflow Skill, $59, 3 days, 1 revision: multi-step workflow skill, conditional logic, light scripting if needed
- Premium, Integrated Skill, $99, 4 days, 1 revision: API/tool integration, multi-source inputs, fuller documentation

Competitive angle:
Most AI automation gigs sell vague consulting. This sells a concrete, owned deliverable: the buyer receives files, docs, and tested outputs.

## Quality Rules

- Do not promise guaranteed revenue, ranking, or sales.
- Do not offer HIPAA, regulated medical, payment-processing, or complex auth work in base packages.
- Require examples before accepting unclear custom orders.
- Push vague or large requests into "message me first" rather than letting buyers self-scope badly.
- Use FAQs and saved replies to keep responses fast and consistent.
