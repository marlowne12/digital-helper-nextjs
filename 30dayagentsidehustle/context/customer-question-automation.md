# Customer Question Automation

Use these as Fiverr saved replies, FAQs, and order-intake triage. The goal is fast answers without accepting vague or oversized work.

## Triage Rules

### Green Light

Reply with the matching package when the buyer provides:
- Clear workflow or website URL
- Specific tools/platforms involved
- Examples of current manual output or desired chatbot answers
- Clear final output

### Needs Clarification

Ask clarifying questions when the buyer says:
- "Can you automate my business?"
- "I need AI for everything"
- "Can you connect all my tools?"
- "Build me a full app"
- "Make ChatGPT rank my site"

### Decline or Custom Scope

Do not accept base package orders for:
- Payment collection inside chatbot
- HIPAA/medical/legal compliance
- Enterprise CRM builds
- Full SaaS apps with auth and database
- Guaranteed SEO, AI rankings, or sales
- Anything requiring secret credentials before scope is agreed
