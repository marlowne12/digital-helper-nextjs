# Manufacturing Vertical

## Overview
The manufacturing page reframes the Digital Helper offer for industrial and B2B buyers.
It emphasizes technical credibility, capability showcase, and RFQ-style lead capture.

## Main promise
Help manufacturers present themselves as serious, high-capability partners and generate better-qualified industrial leads.

## Offer translation
- capability showcases for technical buyers
- B2B lead funnels and qualification
- supply-chain/procurement SEO
- digital forms that streamline inquiry and RFQ processes

## Best-fit themes
This vertical should lean on precision, specs, process clarity, and high-value contract acquisition.
