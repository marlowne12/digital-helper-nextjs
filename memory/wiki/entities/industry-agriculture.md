# Agriculture Vertical

## Overview
The agriculture page positions Digital Helper for agribusiness, farms, suppliers, and processing facilities.
It emphasizes modernization, partner communication, and B2B visibility.

## Main promise
Help traditional agriculture businesses modernize outreach and operations through digital systems that improve communication and make them easier to discover.

## Offer translation
- smart portals for suppliers/partners
- market visibility to reach broader buyers
- automated reporting and logistics communication

## Best-fit themes
This vertical fits businesses that need operational clarity and B2B credibility more than flashy consumer branding.
