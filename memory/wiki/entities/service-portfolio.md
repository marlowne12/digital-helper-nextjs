# Service Portfolio

## Overview
Digital Helper presents a bundled offer set centered on revenue-oriented web presence and AI-enabled growth.
The service mix spans website builds, local SEO, AI automation, lead generation, and reputation management.

## Core services
- Web Design: high-performance, conversion-focused websites using modern frontend technology
- Local SEO: Google Business Profile, local citations, and city/service ranking growth
- AI Automation: chatbot and workflow-style automations framed as a digital workforce
- Lead Generation: pay-per-lead or automated prospecting positioning
- Reputation Management: review monitoring, analysis, and response support

## Pricing signals
Known project memory indicates three public/internal tiers:
- Starter — $1,999: 5-page website, basic SEO
- Professional — $3,999: 10 pages plus AI chatbot
- Enterprise — $997/mo: 24/7 AI sales agent

## Packaging logic
The offer stack appears designed to move prospects from a website need into recurring-value services:
1. website build or redesign
2. local SEO and visibility
3. AI/chat/review tooling
4. ongoing lead generation or reputation support

## Strategic significance
This service portfolio supports the brand claim that Digital Helper is not only a web design shop, but a growth and automation partner for local businesses.
