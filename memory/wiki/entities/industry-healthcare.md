# Healthcare Vertical

## Overview
The healthcare page targets clinics, dental practices, and healthcare providers by centering trust, bookings, and local patient acquisition.

## Main promise
Use secure, professional digital presence and local SEO to build patient confidence and increase appointment flow.

## Offer translation
- trust-building layouts and authority cues
- patient intake and booking workflows
- local search dominance for treatment-intent queries
- review capture and reputation support

## Best-fit themes
This vertical is strongest when paired with proof, reviews, credibility signals, and strong local SEO execution.
