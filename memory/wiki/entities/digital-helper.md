# Digital Helper

## Overview
Digital Helper is the business and website behind `digital-helper.com`.
It presents itself as a modern web design and growth partner with a strong emphasis on local SEO and AI-assisted service delivery.

## Current positioning
- Local-first agency targeting Richland, Kennewick, Pasco, and the broader Tri-Cities area
- Combines web design, SEO, and AI-flavored product features
- Messaging emphasizes practical AI capabilities rather than novelty alone

## Likely target audience
- Small businesses in the Tri-Cities area
- Businesses needing modern websites plus local search visibility
- Prospects interested in AI-enabled workflows or lead capture improvements

## Current strategic themes
- Local SEO domination for city-specific service terms
- AI authority/content positioning
- Growth through structured keyword targeting, competitor monitoring, and campaign execution
