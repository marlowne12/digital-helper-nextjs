# Services vs Verticals

## What is being compared
This comparison contrasts Digital Helper's core service pages with its industry/vertical pages.
The services define what the company does. The verticals define who the offer is for and how the same capabilities should be reframed.

## Comparison table
| Dimension | Service Pages | Vertical Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Primary job | Explain offers | Reduce genericness for specific buyer types |
| Visitor intent | "What do you do?" | "Do you understand my industry?" |
| Messaging style | Capability-first | Context-first |
| SEO role | Commercial service intent | Industry-specific relevance and long-tail breadth |
| Conversion role | Clarify package/value | Increase trust and fit |
| Reuse pattern | Shared core offer language | Customized language per market |

## Main insight
The vertical pages are not separate offers; they are a translation layer over the same service portfolio.
They should not drift into inventing entirely new delivery models unless the business truly supports them.

## Recommended operating rule
- services should stay canonical for what is sold
- verticals should specialize the story, examples, proof, and objections
- every major vertical should point back to the most relevant service pages

## Risks
- duplicated claims without proof
- vertical pages becoming thin rewrites of service pages
- conflict between vertical promises and actual package scope
