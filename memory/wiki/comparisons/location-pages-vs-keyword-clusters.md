# Location Pages vs Keyword Clusters

## What is being compared
This page compares the city/location page system with the project's hero and secondary keyword structure.

## Comparison table
| Dimension | Location Pages | Keyword Clusters |
|---|---|---|
| Primary unit | City/region pages | Search-intent themes |
| Main purpose | Capture geo-intent and local trust | Organize demand across priority terms |
| Best for | Richland, Kennewick, Pasco, West Richland queries | Service terms, qualifiers, AI themes, SMB terms |
| Funnel role | Bottom/mid-funnel local intent | Mixed funnel depending on keyword |
| Optimization style | City proof + local framing | Topic coverage + internal linking |
| Risk | Cannibalization across nearby city pages | Topic sprawl or weak page mapping |

## Mapping insight
The cleanest mapping appears to be:
- hero geo terms -> location pages
- direct commercial service terms -> service pages
- informational/supporting terms -> blog and supporting content
- AI differentiator terms -> AI-related service and feature content

## Practical recommendation
Do not let location pages try to carry every keyword theme.
Use them primarily for geo-specific trust and purchase intent, while service and content pages carry broader thematic coverage.
