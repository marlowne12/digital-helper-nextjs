# Digital Helper vs Competitors

## What is being compared
This page compares Digital Helper's current positioning against the main local competitor set identified in project memory.

## Comparison table
| Competitor | Their strength | Digital Helper advantage | Best counter-positioning |
|---|---|---|---|
| webdesign-pasco.com | local citations and GBP | stronger modern build + AI story | "local authority plus modern conversion systems" |
| tricitieswebdesign.com | content marketing and backlinks | faster, more technical, AI-enabled positioning | "better implementation and stronger product story" |
| crescensivedesign.com | deeper portfolio and e-commerce depth | stronger local focus and responsiveness | "Tri-Cities specialist, not generic regional agency" |
| pixelprodigy.com | speed and budget packaging | quality, support, and AI differentiation | "serious growth partner, not commodity build shop" |
| tricitiesdigital.com | full-service breadth and established brand | SMB focus, agility, AI feature specificity | "more focused, faster, and more practical for local SMBs" |

## Synthesis
Digital Helper appears strongest when it combines four claims at once:
- local-first Tri-Cities specificity
- modern high-performance implementation
- practical AI features embedded into the offer
- SMB-friendly responsiveness and clarity

## Weak points to watch
To fully win this positioning, the site likely needs:
- stronger proof/case studies
- stronger trust signals for higher-stakes industries
- clearer evidence that the AI story is real and not decorative

## Recommended messaging spine
"We help Tri-Cities businesses outgrow brochure websites with high-performance design, local SEO, and practical AI systems that capture and convert leads."
