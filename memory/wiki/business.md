## 🚨 Crisis Communication & Edge Cases

### Negative Review Protocol
1. **Alert:** Automated notification within 15 minutes of new review
2. **Acknowledge:** Human response within 1 hour, moving conversation offline: "Please call me directly at 509-987-5060 so we can fix this immediately."
3. **Resolve:** Fix issue even if not our fault—client perception priority
4. **Follow-up:** Request revision/update after resolution

**Never:** Argue publicly, blame the client, use legal threats.

### Scope Creep Defense
"Absolutely, we can add [feature]. Based on current sprint, that adds [timeframe] and [cost]. Should I send an updated proposal, or can this wait until Phase 2?"

*Always offer paid option OR phased option. Never just "no."*
