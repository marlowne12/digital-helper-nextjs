# Competitor Landscape

## Overview
Digital Helper competes primarily against local or region-adjacent agencies with strengths in citations, content, portfolio depth, or pricing simplicity.
The project's current positioning strategy is to beat them with stronger AI features, modern implementation, and local relevance.

## Main competitors
- webdesign-pasco.com — strongest on local citations and GBP execution
- tricitieswebdesign.com — strongest on content marketing and local backlinks
- crescensivedesign.com — strongest on e-commerce depth and portfolio maturity
- pixelprodigy.com — strongest on speed and budget packaging
- tricitiesdigital.com — strongest on full-service agency breadth and established identity

## Strategic pattern
The competitive story is consistent:
- match or exceed local SEO basics
- win on modern design/technology
- emphasize practical AI differentiation
- stay more focused on SMB needs and responsiveness than larger agencies

## Weaknesses to exploit
Across the competitor set, recurring exploitable gaps include one or more of:
- outdated aesthetics
- weak AI/product story
- slower delivery
- higher pricing
- less SMB specificity
- less local focus
