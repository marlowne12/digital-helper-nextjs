# Local SEO Strategy

## Overview
Digital Helper's SEO strategy is centered on dominating Tri-Cities local-intent search terms, especially web design queries tied to Richland, Kennewick, Pasco, and West Richland.

## Main objective
Claim top local positions for city-plus-service queries that map directly to web design and SEO purchase intent.

## Hero-keyword thesis
Priority-one terms are exact or near-exact location/service phrases such as:
- web design Richland WA
- Richland WA web design
- Tri-Cities web designer
- website design Kennewick
- Pasco WA web developer

These terms are the clearest revenue keywords and anchor the location-page strategy.

## Supporting motions
- optimize dedicated location pages for each city
- strengthen Google Business Profile visibility
- build local citations and directory mentions
- publish supporting informational content around local SEO and related service demand
- connect service pages and location pages into stronger topic clusters
