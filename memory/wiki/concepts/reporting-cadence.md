# Reporting Cadence

## Overview
The reporting system is organized into monthly, quarterly, and campaign-specific layers.
This creates a useful separation between tactical performance tracking and strategic planning.

## Monthly reports
Monthly reports are expected to track:
- executive summary
- traffic metrics
- keyword rankings
- backlinks
- content performance
- lead generation
- next-month priorities

This is the core operational review loop.

## Quarterly reports
Quarterly reports are meant to cover:
- quarter overview
- goal progress
- budget vs actual
- competitor movement
- strategic recommendations
- resource allocation

This is the strategic review layer.

## Campaign reports
Campaign reports track named initiatives such as:
- Tri-Cities Local Dominance
- AI Authority Building

These reports are the bridge between strategy and execution, measuring campaign-specific milestones, blockers, and metrics.

## Why this matters
The reporting structure implies the team wants a disciplined marketing operating system, not just ad hoc content or SEO activity.
It supports recurring review and prioritization.
