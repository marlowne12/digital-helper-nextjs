# Vertical Market Strategy

## Overview
Digital Helper uses industry pages to make its service offer feel specialized rather than generic.
The current vertical set covers agriculture, healthcare, manufacturing, retail/e-commerce, and wineries.

## Strategic role
These pages let the business translate the same core capabilities into industry-specific language:
- trust and booking for healthcare
- B2B capability and RFQ flow for manufacturing
- online sales and retention for retail/e-commerce
- tourism, brand story, and reservations for wineries
- partner communication and reporting for agriculture

## Core pattern
Across all verticals, the same underlying engine appears repeatedly:
- modern site design
- SEO or discoverability
- automation / workflow support
- conversion-oriented digital systems

This means the vertical strategy is mainly a packaging and positioning layer built on top of the shared service portfolio.

## Why this matters
Industry pages can improve:
- conversion by reducing perceived genericness
- SEO breadth through vertical-specific terminology
- sales conversations by giving prospects a relevant entry point

## Risks to watch
- pages becoming too templated and thin
- weak proof for industries with no case studies yet
- keyword overlap with service and location pages
