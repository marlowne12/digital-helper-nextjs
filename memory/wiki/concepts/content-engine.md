# Content Engine

## Overview
The content plan is structured to support both local-intent rankings and AI-authority positioning.
It mixes blog posts, service pages, location pages, and case-study style assets.

## Publishing structure
The calendar shows a deliberate pattern:
- blog posts for educational and supporting-intent search coverage
- service pages for direct commercial-intent keywords
- location pages for city-based ranking goals
- case studies for proof and conversion support

## High-value planned content themes
- website redesign pain and conversion recovery
- AI website builder / AI web design authority
- local SEO guidance for Tri-Cities businesses
- city-based web design landing pages
- reputation management and lead-generation proof assets

## Strategic role
The content engine is the bridge between keyword strategy and authority-building.
It also provides proof points and topical breadth that competitors using thinner service pages may lack.

## Maintenance notes
The calendar recommends:
- publishing blogs midweek
- refreshing location pages quarterly
- refreshing service pages every 6 months
- monitoring ranking impact after each content piece
