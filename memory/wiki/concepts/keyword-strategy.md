# Keyword Strategy

## Overview
The keyword system is split into hero and secondary targets.
Hero terms are revenue-critical local-intent phrases; secondary terms expand reach around services, affordability, SMB intent, and AI differentiation.

## Hero keywords
The hero set is dominated by local web design phrases with strong purchase intent and clear geographic modifiers.
These should map closely to location pages, homepage messaging, and core service pages.

## Secondary keywords
The secondary set broadens the funnel with terms such as:
- local seo Richland
- seo company Tri-Cities
- ai website builder
- small business seo
- website developer Richland
- web design company Tri-Cities
- affordable web design Richland
- professional web design Pasco

## Notable insight
`ai website builder` has the highest listed volume among the secondary set and appears to be the most obvious bridge between SEO demand and Digital Helper's AI positioning.

## Content implications
- service pages should target commercial-intent terms
- blog posts should target educational or supporting-intent terms
- location pages should capture geo-modified hero terms
- pricing and differentiation content should support qualifier keywords like affordability, professionalism, and SMB fit
