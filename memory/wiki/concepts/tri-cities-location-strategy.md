# Tri-Cities Location Strategy

## Overview
Digital Helper's location architecture is built around a hub page for the Tri-Cities region plus dedicated city pages for Richland, Kennewick, Pasco, and West Richland.
This is the practical execution layer for the broader local SEO strategy.

## Structure
- `/locations` acts as the regional hub
- `/locations/richland` targets the home-market and highest-trust local positioning
- `/locations/kennewick` targets the commercial center and high-intent business traffic
- `/locations/pasco` targets the fastest-growth city and expanding demand
- `/locations/west-richland` targets residential growth and service-business opportunity

## City-by-city positioning
### Richland
- strongest credibility angle because the agency is Richland-based
- messaging leans on local familiarity and neighborhood knowledge
- likely flagship location page

### Kennewick
- framed as the commercial heart of the Tri-Cities
- strongest angle for traffic, shopping corridors, and lead conversion
- likely important for broader business-service terms

### Pasco
- framed around rapid growth and expanding market demand
- good fit for growth messaging and underserved demand capture

### West Richland
- framed around residential/family growth and local loyalty
- strong fit for home-service and specialist businesses

## Strategic role
These pages let Digital Helper express the same service offering with location-specific proof, vocabulary, and market framing.
They should be tightly connected to service pages and keyword clusters.

## Recommended maintenance focus
- keep city-specific testimonials, proof, or references distinct where possible
- connect each city page to matching service pages
- monitor keyword overlap/cannibalization between city pages
- refresh stats and local details periodically so pages stay believable
