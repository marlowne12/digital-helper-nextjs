## 3) Value-first spec site offer

**Subject ideas:**
- I sketched an idea for your website
- Put together a homepage concept for [Business Name]
- A quick mockup idea for your business

Hi [First Name],

I was checking out local businesses in the Tri-Cities and spent a little time thinking about how [Business Name] could look online.

I had a few ideas for a cleaner, higher-converting website presence, so I wanted to reach out before I went any further.

What I can do is put together a simple spec homepage concept for your business so you can actually see the direction instead of guessing from a sales pitch. That usually makes it easier to decide whether a new site is even worth considering.

If you are open to it, I can mock up a simple concept based on your services, service area, and the type of customers you want more of.

If it’s not useful, no worries. If you want me to do it, just reply with “mockup” and I’ll send one over.

Best,
[Your Name]
Digital Helper
[Phone]
[Website]

## Notes

- Keep the first email short and plain text.
- Personalize with business name, city, and one real observation.
- Avoid sounding automated or overly polished.
- Best used with a light follow-up 3 to 5 business days later.
