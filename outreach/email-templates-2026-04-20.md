## Personalization Notes

To keep these from feeling mass-sent, customize at least 2 to 3 details before sending:
- business name
- city/service area
- primary service offered
- something specific you noticed, like no website, outdated site, weak Google profile, or unclear messaging
- sender name and contact info

## Tone Guidelines

Keep the message:
- short
- specific
- useful
- low pressure
- focused on helping, not closing

Avoid:
- hypey claims
- fake urgency
- long lists of services
- sounding automated or overly polished
