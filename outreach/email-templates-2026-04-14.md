## Usage Notes

- **Personalize** the `[brackets]` fields before sending
- **Keep follow-ups light** — one polite reminder, then let it rest
- **Avoid:** excessive exclamation points, vague claims ("We're the best!"), and aggressive CTAs
- **Tone goal:** Be the helpful neighbor, not the pushy salesperson
