## Notes

- Keep personalization light but real: mention city, business name, and one specific observation.
- Best fit: plumbers, electricians, landscapers, cleaners, roofers, HVAC, med spas, contractors.
- Tone goal: neighborly, competent, helpful.
- Avoid sounding automated or overly polished.
