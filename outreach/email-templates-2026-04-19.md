# Digital Helper Outreach Email Templates

Date: 2026-04-19
Audience: Local service businesses
Tone: Conversational, helpful, not salesy

---

## 1) Cold outreach for businesses without websites

**Subject ideas:**
- Quick note about your online presence
- Noticed your business may not have a website
- Small idea to help more local customers find you

Hi [First Name],

I came across [Business Name] and wanted to reach out because I noticed you may not have a website set up yet, or at least not one that is easy for customers to find.

A lot of local businesses are still getting most of their work from referrals, which is great, but many potential customers still look you up before they call. If they cannot quickly find your services, hours, reviews, or a simple way to contact you, there is a good chance they move on to the next option.

I run Digital Helper Agency here in the Tri-Cities area, and we help local businesses get simple, clean websites up without making the process a headache.

If it would be useful, I would be happy to send over a few quick ideas for what a basic high-converting site for [Business Name] could include. No pressure, just helpful suggestions.

Either way, I hope business is going well.

Best,
[Your Name]
Digital Helper Agency
[Phone]
[Website]

## Optional personalization lines

Use 1 to 2 of these near the top to make the email feel more real:

- I saw you serve the [City] area and wanted to reach out locally.
- I noticed you have solid reviews, which is a great foundation.
- I came across your business while looking at local service providers in [City].
- It looks like most of your business probably comes from referrals, which usually means there is untapped search traffic available.
- I noticed your Google presence is there, but it may not be doing as much as it could yet.

## Notes for sending

- Keep the email short.
- Personalize the first 2 lines.
- Do not stack too many claims.
- End with one easy, low-pressure call to action.
- Best fit industries: contractors, landscapers, roofers, plumbers, electricians, cleaning companies, med spas, dentists, and auto detailers.
