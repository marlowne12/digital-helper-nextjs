## Notes for personalization

- Mention the business name and city early.
- Keep the first line specific so it does not feel mass sent.
- Do not over-explain services.
- Offer one clear next step only.
- Keep tone helpful, local, and low-pressure.
- If possible, reference one real observation, like no website, weak Google profile, outdated branding, or missing calls to action.
